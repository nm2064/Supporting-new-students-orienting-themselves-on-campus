# Knowledge Base - University Information

## Campus Location
The main campus is located at 123 University Avenue, Academic City, AC 12345.
The campus is accessible via public transportation, with the University Metro Station located just 500 meters from the main entrance.

## Library Hours
The University Library is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
On weekends, the library operates from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
During exam periods, the library extends its hours and remains open 24/7 for the final two weeks of each semester.
The library offers quiet study zones, group study rooms, and access to over 500,000 digital resources.

## Computer Science Department
The Computer Science Department offers undergraduate and graduate programs.
Undergraduate degrees include Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Bachelor of Science in Data Science.
Graduate programs include Master's in Computer Science and Ph.D. in Computer Science.
The department is located in the Science Building, rooms 201-300.
Department office hours are Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## IT Support
For technical support, contact the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@university.edu or call (555) 123-4567.
The IT Help Desk is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
For after-hours emergencies, use the online ticket system at support.university.edu.
Common services include password resets, Wi-Fi troubleshooting, and software installation support.

## Student Housing
On-campus housing is available for undergraduate and graduate students.
The residence halls include:
- North Hall: First-year students, shared rooms
- South Hall: Upperclassmen, suite-style living
- Graduate Village: Apartments for graduate students and families
Applications for housing open on February 1 for the following academic year.
Priority is given to students who apply before March 15.

## Dining Services
The university operates five dining halls and twelve cafes across campus.
Meal plans are available for resident and commuter students.
The main dining hall, University Commons, is open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
Special dietary accommodations including vegetarian, vegan, halal, and kosher options are available at all locations.

## Parking Information
Student parking permits cost $150 per semester.
Faculty and staff permits cost $200 per year.
Visitor parking is available in Lot A at $2 per hour.
Parking enforcement operates Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
All vehicles must display a valid permit to avoid fines.

## Health Services
The University Health Center provides medical care for all enrolled students.
Services include primary care, mental health counseling, vaccinations, and wellness programs.
The Health Center is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Appointments can be scheduled online at health.university.edu or by calling (555) 987-6543.
Emergency services are available 24/7 through the campus safety escort program.
